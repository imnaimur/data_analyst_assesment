Q9. THE BUDGET
===============
Finance can release BDT 70,000 for next month. It must cover all one-
time costs and the salary of any new agents for that month. How would
you use it, and what do you expect it to return?


ASSUMPTIONS
============
1. Fixed agent = BDT 650/day (16,900 / 26); monthly = 650 × 30 = BDT 19,500.
2. Variable agent = 300 + 14 × deliveries/day; at 32 deliveries = BDT 748/day.
   Breakeven: 650 = 300 + 14d → d = 25. Hubs needing agents are above 25, so Fixed is cheaper.
3. One-time hiring costs (costs tab of cost_sheet.xlsx) are not in the dataset.
   Three scenarios are shown (BDT 5,000 / 10,000 / 15,500 per agent).
4. Avg delivery charge = BDT 65/order (orders table, 22,789 orders).
5. Transfers between hubs add zero salary cost — agents are already on payroll.
6. 50% Late Dispatch reduction at Riverside is an operational target; conservative (30%) noted.


THE BUDGET CONSTRAINT
======================
The 8-agent request costs BDT 156,000 in salary alone — 2.2× the entire
budget. Even 5 agents (Q7 gap) costs BDT 97,500. What can BDT 70,000
actually buy?

  One-time cost/agent | Max Fixed hires | Remaining
  --------------------+-----------------+----------
  BDT 0               |               3 | BDT 11,500
  BDT 5,000           |               2 | BDT 21,000
  BDT 10,000          |               2 | BDT 11,000
  BDT 15,500          |               2 |  BDT 1,000

At any realistic one-time cost the budget supports exactly 2 hires.
Hiring alone cannot close the gap — the company must combine hiring
with zero-cost moves.


THE PLAN
=========

MOVE 1 — Transfer 3 agents from Lakeview (BDT 0)

Lakeview has 11 agents but needs only 8 (Q7). At 56.2% utilisation (Q2)
its agents deliver just 20.4 orders/day each (Q1) — not because they
are slow, but because there are not enough orders. Its cost per delivery,
BDT 31.06, is the highest in the company (Q6) purely due to idle
overhead. Moving 3 of its 8 Fixed agents to Greenfield (2) and
Hillcrest (1) fixes both problems at once: the receiving hubs get the
agents they need, and Lakeview's cost per delivery drops from BDT 31.06
to ~BDT 22.15 (−29%) by right-sizing to 8 agents.

The transferred agents move from 20.4 deliveries/day each at Lakeview
to 32–35/day at the busier hubs (Q1 Week 2 averages: Greenfield 32.0,
Hillcrest 34.6). The gain:

  Location    | Agents | Del/agent/day | Del/day | Del/month
  ------------+--------+---------------+---------+----------
  At Lakeview |      3 |          20.4 |    61.2 |    1,836
  At new hubs |      3 |          32.9 |    98.6 |    2,958
  ------------+--------+---------------+---------+----------
  NET GAIN    |        |               |   +37.4 |   +1,122

  Revenue: 1,122 × BDT 65 = BDT 72,930/month.  Cost: BDT 0.

MOVE 2 — Hire 2 Fixed agents (BDT 39,000 salary + one-time costs)

After the transfers, two gaps remain. Hillcrest is still short by 1 —
at 95.9% utilisation (Q2) and 34.6 deliveries/agent/day (Q1) its
agents are already at the planning ceiling. Riverside is short by 1,
though that gap is caused by dispatch waste, not volume (Q3/Q8); the
hire is a buffer while operations are fixed.

  Hub       | Attempts/day | ÷ Att/order | = Del/day | × 30 days | Del/month | × BDT 65  | Revenue
  ----------+--------------+-------------+-----------+-----------+-----------+-----------+--------
  Hillcrest |           34 |       1.078 |      31.5 |       945 |       945 | BDT 65    | 61,425
  Riverside |           34 |       1.351 |      25.2 |       756 |       756 | BDT 65    | 49,140
  ----------+--------------+-------------+-----------+-----------+-----------+-----------+--------
  TOTAL     |              |             |           |           |     1,701 |           | 110,565

  Cost: 2 × BDT 19,500 = BDT 39,000.  Net: BDT 71,565/month.
  Payback: 39,000 / (110,565 / 30) = 10.6 days.

MOVE 3 — Riverside dispatch programme (remainder: BDT 0–21,000)

Riverside's 312 Late Dispatch holds per week (Q3) each trigger a re-
attempt the next day. At BDT 19.12 per attempt (650 / 34), this wastes
BDT 5,965/week — BDT 25,650/month — more than the salary of one new
agent. If the programme halves Late Dispatch:

  Metric                   | Calculation              | Result
  -------------------------+--------------------------+----------------
  Freed attempts/month     | 156/wk × 4.3            |            671
  Additional deliveries    | 671 / 1.075              |            624
  Revenue                  | 624 × BDT 65             | BDT 40,560
  Agent-time savings       | 671 × BDT 19.12          | BDT 12,830
  -------------------------+--------------------------+----------------
  Combined monthly benefit |                          | BDT 53,390
  Programme cost (one-time)|                          | ≤ BDT 21,000

  At a conservative 30% reduction the benefit is still ~BDT 32,000/month.

RISKS
======
1. High one-time costs may eliminate the dispatch programme budget.
   The 2 hires + 3 transfers still stand alone; dispatch waits a cycle.
2. Transfers blocked → hire a 3rd agent (BDT 58,500 salary); feasible
   only at low one-time costs.
3. Forecast misses → exposure limited to BDT 39,000/month (vs
   BDT 156,000 for 8 hires).
4. Ramp-up takes 1–2 weeks → at 80% target, net still positive
   (BDT 49,450 from hires alone).
5. Dispatch fix underperforms → at 30% reduction, benefit still
   ~BDT 32,000/month against ≤BDT 21,000 cost.


FINAL ANSWER
=============
Hire 2 Fixed agents (Hillcrest + Riverside): BDT 39,000 salary + one-
time costs. Transfer 3 from Lakeview to Greenfield (2) and Hillcrest
(1): BDT 0. Invest the remainder in Riverside's Late Dispatch fix.

  New headcount: 2     Budget: BDT 70,000     ROI: 253%
  Extra deliveries: 3,447/month     Net return: ≥ BDT 176,885

The budget is too small for 8 hires and too valuable to spend only on
salary. Two agents where they matter most, three redeployed at zero
cost, and one targeted operational fix — every gap closed, every hub
balanced, and the investment repaid within two weeks.
